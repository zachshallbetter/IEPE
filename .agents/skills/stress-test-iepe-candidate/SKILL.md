# Stress Test an IEPE Candidate

Read `docs/EPISTEMIC_STRESS_TESTING.md` from the pinned IEPE source. Freeze the qualified candidate before revealing designed perturbations. Define protected invariants, surprise budget, evaluator, evidence, and stop conditions.

Use sealed World Cards when the test depends on withheld conditions. Record each result as robust, adaptable, bounded, fragile, unsafe, or unknown. Update the unknown-variable ledger and robustness profile.

Do not use synthetic trials to claim human outcomes. Stop when a perturbation crosses consent, safety, identity, factual, or promotion boundaries.
