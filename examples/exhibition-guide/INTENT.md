# Project Intent

## Purpose

Help a first-time visitor enter a small exhibition with enough orientation to make an independent choice about where to begin.

## Human outcome

The visitor understands that there is no required route, recognizes three possible ways to enter the exhibition, and feels permitted to follow personal curiosity.

## Values

- Visitor agency
- Plain language
- Interpretive openness
- Physical and cognitive accessibility
- Respect for uncertainty

## Prohibited outcomes

- Presenting one route as authoritative
- Explaining what an artwork is supposed to mean
- Requiring prior knowledge
- Using urgency or completion pressure
- Claiming that the guide has been validated with visitors
