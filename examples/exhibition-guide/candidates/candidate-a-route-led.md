# Candidate A: Route-Led

## Begin Here

Start with the suspended glass work in the first room. Then continue to the projection beyond the central partition. Finish beside the three drawings near the quiet seating area.

The exhibition follows water as material, memory, and movement. Each section develops the idea introduced by the one before it. Allow about twenty minutes for the complete route.

If you have less time, visit the glass work and the final drawings. Labels beside each work provide additional context.

You may return to the entrance at any point.
