# Candidate B: Choice-Led

## Choose Where to Begin

There is no required route through **Ways of Seeing Water**. Begin with the question that feels closest to you.

### What does water hold?

Find the three drawings near the quiet seating area.

### How does water change what we see?

Look for the suspended glass work in the first room.

### What happens when water becomes time?

Enter the projection space beyond the central partition.

Move between these works in any order. You can follow one question, change direction, pause, or leave a section unfinished. Labels offer context, but you do not need them to begin.
