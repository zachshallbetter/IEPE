# Negative Results

## Candidate A: Route-Led

Candidate A was rejected for the pilot baseline because it instructs visitors to start, continue, and finish in a curatorial sequence. This contradicts the declared intent to support independent entry and makes the later statement about returning to the entrance insufficient.

The direction is not universally invalid. It could be appropriate for an exhibition whose meaning depends on sequence. It is invalid under this project’s current intent and experience contract.

The result is retained so future revisions do not repeatedly rediscover that a friendly tone alone does not establish visitor agency.
